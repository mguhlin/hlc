Grade 7 — Texas History

Problem-Based Learning units for Grade 7 Texas History. Each unit moves students from building knowledge (surface) to organizing it (deep) to solving a real, ill-structured problem as a stakeholder (transfer).

① Surface

Build the knowledge

Vocabulary, key facts, jigsaw reading, map work.

② Deep

Connect & organize

Cause–effect concept map, primary sources, points of view, argument.

③ Transfer

Solve the problem

Meet the problem, take a role, investigate, propose a solution, debrief.

Featured · Texas History · §113.19

Spindletop, 1901 — When Oil Changed Everything

January 1901. The Lucas Gusher near Beaumont has struck oil — more than anyone has ever seen. Students become stakeholders in a booming town and decide how the community should handle the rush so it lasts. 5–8 class periods.

Dust and Drought, 1935 — Stay or Go?

1935 on the Texas High Plains. Drought, dust storms, and Depression debt are failing your family's farm. Students become stakeholders and decide what the family — and the community and government — should do about the Dust Bowl. 5–8 class periods.

Open the unit → Texas History · §113.19

One Star or Many? 1845 — Join the United States?

1845. The Republic of Texas is independent but deep in debt and unsure of its future. Students become stakeholders and decide whether Texas should accept annexation and join the United States. 5–8 class periods.
