Privacy & Compliance

[Your Suite] collects nothing. There are no accounts, no logins, and no analytics. Everything runs in the student's browser; session state (which locks are open) lives only on that device and disappears when the tab closes.

🔒 No student data leaves the browser. The single outside request is to Google Fonts for typography — remove it by self-hosting the fonts for zero external egress. Every page sets <meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">.

Accessibility

Built toward WCAG 2.1 AA: semantic headings, keyboard-operable locks, decorative icons hidden from assistive tech, descriptive alt text on images, logical CSS properties for right-to-left languages, and a seven-language switcher. Verify against your own assistive-technology stack before adoption.

This statement describes the design intent of a self-contained activity and is not legal advice. Confirm compliance with your district's privacy officer before adoption.
