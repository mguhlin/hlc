About these maps

History Event Maps is a library of 24 reusable one-page graphic organizers for social studies in grades 3–5, 6–8, and 9–12.

How to use a map

Choose a grade band and a thinking skill, preview the blank map on screen, then print it or download the PDF.

TEKS Skill Focus

Aligned to the current 2022-adopted Texas Social Studies TEKS implemented beginning in 2024-2025.

Grades 3-5: chronology, cause/effect, sources, point of view, maps, graphic organizers, claims and evidence.

Grades 6-8: research, chronology, cause/effect, comparison, historical context, perspective, source evaluation, visual communication.

Grades 9-12: historical inquiry, chronology, causation, perspective, source evaluation, claims/evidence, geographic interpretation, visual communication.

Privacy

No accounts, cookies, forms, or analytics. The only thing stored in your browser is your language choice.

Accessibility

Every label is real text, so it reflows, translates, and reads aloud with a screen reader.
