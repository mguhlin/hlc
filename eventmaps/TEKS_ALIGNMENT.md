# TEKS Alignment Reference

## How to use this alignment

These 24 event maps are reusable graphic organizers rather than event-specific lessons. Their TEKS alignment therefore emphasizes the recurring **Social Studies Skills** standards that apply when students gather information from sources, sequence events, analyze cause and effect, compare perspectives, interpret geography, evaluate evidence, and communicate conclusions.

The historical topic assigned by the teacher determines the separate **content TEKS** for that activity.

## Important implementation note

Do not treat a generic map as proof that a particular content TEKS has been taught. Pair the map with the content standard for the event, era, person, government topic, geographic concept, or economic concept students are studying.
