Juneteenth & the Meaning of Freedom

Primary-source investigations, timelines, and celebrations that trace June 19, 1865, from Galveston to a federal holiday. Students read General Order No. 3, weigh what freedom meant in practice, and connect the past to how Texans mark the day today.

13 activitiesGrades K–12Seven languages

How this collection works

  • No logins, no data collected, works offline.
  • Seven languages, with a printable path for every activity.
  • Teacher supports in each activity: TEKS, answer key, and discussion questions.
  • Built to teach inside your Civil War and Reconstruction units — not reserved for June.

TEKS alignment and timing

Activities align to the TEKS in force now. The State Board of Education completed its K–12 social studies revision in September 2026; districts begin implementing the new standards in 2030–31 for the early grades, with high-school courses following in 2033–34. Each activity carries a blank 2026 TEKS field to complete once crosswalks are published.
