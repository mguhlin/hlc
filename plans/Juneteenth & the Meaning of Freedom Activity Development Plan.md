# Juneteenth & the Meaning of Freedom: Activity Development Plan

Oct 17, 2026 · @Miguel Guhlin

## Overview

Build a new activity area for the [Learning Activities Hub](https://mglearn.github.io/activities/): 13 self-contained, browser-based Juneteenth activities across four grade bands, each tied to a verified TEKS student expectation. The source list is NEA's [Teaching Juneteenth and the Meaning of Freedom](https://www.nea.org/professional-excellence/student-engagement/tools-tips/teaching-juneteenth-and-meaning-freedom), which argues Juneteenth belongs inside regular American history lessons, not only on the holiday.

**Hub card copy (draft):** Grades K–12 · 13 activities · Seven languages. *Juneteenth & the Meaning of Freedom.* Primary-source investigations, timelines, and celebrations that trace June 19, 1865, from Galveston to a federal holiday. Students read General Order No. 3, weigh what freedom meant in practice, and connect the past to how Texans celebrate today. Open the collection →

**Design principles (match the hub):**

- No logins, no data collected, works offline
- Seven languages, printable path for every activity
- Teacher supports in every activity: TEKS, answer key, discussion questions
- Taught inside existing Civil War and Reconstruction units, not reserved for June

The hub's Critical Thinking Breakouts already include a July 5 & Black Freedom Holidays set. Link to it from this collection and avoid duplicating its content.

## TEKS alignment and timing

Align to the current TEKS now; they stay in force until the 2026 TEKS roll out. The SBOE [finished the K–12 revision](https://sboe.texas.gov/state-board-of-education/sboe-news/texas-state-board-of-education-completes-k-12-social-studies-teks-revision-adopts-new-personal-financial-literacy-standards) in September 2026. Districts begin implementing it in [2030–31](https://thetexan.news/issues/education/sboe-approves-new-high-school-social-studies-standards-amid-opposition-from-muslim-attendees/article_fff45a3f-948f-41dd-8bd4-886c6769c40e.html), starting with the early grades; high school courses follow in [2033–34](https://texasscorecard.com/state/sboe-approves-new-high-school-social-studies-standards/). Each activity carries a blank 2026 TEKS field to fill once crosswalks are published.

High school U.S. History begins at 1877, so the 9–12 band frames Juneteenth through its long commemoration: the 1979 state holiday and the federal holiday.

## Development roadmap

Build the 6–8 band first: it has the strongest verified TEKS fit and a ready-made tiered source in the UNT lesson. Each phase ends at a gate before the next begins.

1. **Verify alignment.** Confirm the Grade 2 and §113.41 codes against TEA's current TEKS. Gate: every activity row has a confirmed code or is cut.
2. **Assemble source packs.** Pull primary sources and permissions per band from the NEA list and the Portal to Texas History. Gate: every excerpt has a citation and a reuse check.
3. **Prototype 6–8.** Build General Order No. 3 Close Read and The News Delay as single-file HTML. Gate: one teacher pilot with feedback.
4. **Build remaining bands.** K–2, 3–5, then 9–12, reusing the 6–8 templates.
5. **Translate and print.** Produce seven-language versions and grayscale print paths. Gate: native-speaker spot check per language.
6. **Publish to the hub.** Add the activity-area card, link the existing July 5 & Black Freedom Holidays breakouts, and update hub totals.

Open question: release timing. Release when Grade 4, 7, and 8 teachers reach Civil War and Reconstruction in district scope and sequence, or in spring ahead of Juneteenth itself.
